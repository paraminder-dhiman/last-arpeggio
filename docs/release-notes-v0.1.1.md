A small update to The Last Arpeggio.

**Download** `TheLastArpeggio-v0.1.1-windows.zip` below, unzip it and run `TheLastArpeggio.exe`. To keep your songs and settings, copy the `songs` folder and `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one.

### Fixes
- The switch from the countdown to the live wheel, note and bar no longer makes the Now playing screen jump, which could show a torn frame for a moment
- After stopping a song, the progress bar and clock now stop at the same time the result shows

### Other
- The README now links to the project's page, with a demo video and a breakdown of how it works
